# Now Playing

What Spotify or Music is playing. Spotify is asked first, then Music, and the widget
says nothing when neither is playing.

The first time it runs, macOS asks whether Gailan may control those apps. Say yes or
there is nothing to read. If you say no and change your mind, it is under System
Settings, Privacy & Security, Automation.

Reads every five seconds. A track lasts minutes, and asking a player what it is
playing is not free.
