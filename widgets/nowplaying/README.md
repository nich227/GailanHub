# Now Playing

What Spotify or Music is playing. Spotify is asked first, then Music, and the widget
says nothing when neither is playing.

The first time it runs, macOS asks whether Gailan may control those apps. Say yes or
there is nothing to read. If you say no and change your mind, it is under System
Settings, Privacy & Security, Automation.

The cover comes from Spotify, which gives an address for it. Music holds its artwork as
data rather than at an address, so there is nothing to point at and the square is left
empty there. It is the one thing in the widget that is not monochrome: album art is what
you are looking at, so it is shown as it is rather than made to match the case.

The lamp beside the heading says which of three things is true. A track running: dark
red, beating. A track held: a lighter red, still. Nothing loaded at all: grey, light
against the dark panel and dark against the light one, because there is nothing to be red
about. If you have asked your machine for less movement, the dark red stays steady.

`sample-cover.jpg` is nobody's record. It is there so the screenshot shows the layout
without shipping someone's album art, and so regenerating the preview gives the same
picture every time.

Reads every five seconds. A track lasts minutes, and asking a player what it is
playing is not free.

## Controls

Previous, play or pause, and next, drawn a pixel at a time on a grid so they look built
rather than illustrated. They talk to whichever player the widget is reading, and they
need interaction turned on in Gailan's preferences: a button nothing can click is a
picture of a button.

The middle one shows two bars while a track runs and a triangle while it is held.

## Settings

- **Drag to move** lets you hold the panel and put it where you like. Where you leave it
  is remembered.
